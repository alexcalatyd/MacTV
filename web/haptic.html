Haptics diagnostic

Apple closed off the programmatic path in iOS 26.5. The two tests marked key are the ones that decide whether this can work on your iPhone. The other three use the closed path and are here only to confirm the diagnosis.

key 1Real switch, with your finger

A normal <input type="checkbox" switch>. Drag it with your finger. If this doesn't vibrate, iOS doesn't give the web haptics on your device and there's nothing to code around.

key 2Invisible switch over a button

This is the only technique that survives iOS 26.5, and the one that would need to go into the remote: a real, invisible switch covering the button, so your finger actually activates it. Press the button.
